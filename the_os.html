How to use The OS.

"Every action is a vote for who you are becoming."

Five tabs at the bottom. Your constitution is yours to author. The Mentor knows the full picture.

1
Your constitution is yours to author. Open the Constitution tab and edit any virtue — change the name, behaviors, rules. Add new ones. Make dormant the ones you're not working on.
2
Behaviors are the daily reps. Small, concrete, doable today. Each one is a vote for the man you're becoming.
3
Rules are the lines he stands by. Short. Declarative. They show up in the Mentor's context and on Today.
4
Active virtues show up on Today. No weekly rotation, no forced rhythm. You decide what's active.
5
The Mentor knows your full constitution. Active and dormant. Speak to it as you would a steady older brother who knows you.
6
The Lab is for experiments. The Mind is for knowledge. Unchanged.
