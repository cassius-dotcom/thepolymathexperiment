How to use The OS.

"Every action is a vote for who you are becoming."

Five tabs at the bottom. The OS is a rapid-access reference for the man you are becoming.

1
Today is the homepage. Your active virtues. Tap any one to read its full portrait — identity line, behaviors, mantras, rules, anti-behaviors, triggers, habits.
2
Rules is the standing law. Every rule across every virtue, plus standalone general rules. Tap any rule to edit it inline.
3
Constitution is where you author. Edit virtues, add components, change order. Behaviors stay on top; everything else is yours to arrange.
4
Six component types per virtue. Behaviors. Mantras. Rules. Anti-behaviors. Triggers. Habits. Use the ones you need.
5
The Lab is for experiments. The Mind is for knowledge. Unchanged.
